# Questions

Design and setup questions met while building, with the choice made so work could carry on (CLAUDE.md rule 6). **Open** means the designer hasn't confirmed it; **settled** means they have, or it's a plain fact.

| # | Question | Choice made | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | The brief lays out a repository root, but the project lives in `the-curator/` inside a multi-project repo. | All paths in the docs are relative to `the-curator/`. The solution, `src/`, `game/` and `docs/` sit there. | settled |
| Q2 | What happens to the designer's existing skeleton (`project.godot`, `node_2d.tscn`, `Magical_Library.png`)? | `project.godot` and the icon moved into `game/` (the brief's Godot root). The app name is now "The Curator", and the assembly is now `Curator.Godot` to match the brief's csproj name. `node_2d.tscn` gave way to `scenes/Main.tscn`. `Magical_Library.png` is now a mood reference in `docs/reference/` and isn't used in the game; the designer confirmed this. | settled |
| Q3 | Which target framework? Godot 4.6 generates `net8.0` projects, but .NET 8 support ends in November 2026 and the designer's other projects use `net10.0`. | `net10.0` for Core, the tests and the Godot project. Godot 4.6.3 ran it fine with only the .NET 10 runtime installed (Godot loads the newest installed runtime). **Needs the .NET 10 SDK on the designer's PC.** To go back to `net8.0`, change all three csproj files together. | open |
| Q4 | A 1920×1080 window is as big as a 1080p screen, so the taskbar hides part of it. | The viewport stays 1920×1080 (all layout uses those coordinates). The window opens at 1600×900 and scales the picture. Fullscreen comes with the settings menu in M5. | open |
| Q5 | A4 assumes the work happens on the designer's Windows PC with Godot installed; it happened in a Linux cloud container. | Everything was verified with the Linux .NET build of Godot 4.6.3: headless runs for bots and smoke tests, and Xvfb with software rendering for screenshots. Windows-only parts (the `d3d12` driver, the Windows export preset) haven't been tried. | open |
| Q6 | The designer asked for interim art made with Easel (`easel-paint`), Claude painting stroke by stroke, not generated images. Where does it go, given `game/art/painted/` is the designer's? | Easel paintings go in `game/art/easel/<slotId>.png`. Each art slot looks for `painted/` first, then `easel/`, then falls back to the flat procedural placeholder. Deleting an Easel file brings back the placeholder; dropping in a photographed painting overrides both. | settled |
| Q7 | GDD §3 says the last patron arrives at dusk, but the brief (§5.1) advances the phase after each decision. With three patrons, the last one then arrives in the afternoon and leaves at dusk. | Followed the brief. Days 2–7 go morning → midday → afternoon → dusk, and night at the evening. The "day-2 visit at dusk" screenshot will show the third visit's farewell. | open |
| Q8 | What starts the next morning? The command list has none. | `CloseForTheDay` runs the evening and then the next morning in one go (or `WeekEnded` after day 7). The day summary can therefore show tomorrow's exact mana, and the autosave at the evening resumes at the next morning, as §7.12 asks. | open |
| Q9 | Books returned overnight "wait in the tray to be shelved". Can they be lent before they're shelved? | Yes. A returned book is in the library at once, and the catalogue marks it "back today". Shelving it from the tray is a click in the presentation only, not a rule. | open |
| Q10 | How long do the "note it" moments last (A6)? | A loan can be noted from the moment it's made until the next patron is called or the day ends. A return can be noted at any time on the day the book came back. Noting a return also writes the loan's row if it wasn't noted at lending: one click writes the true entry. | open |
| Q11 | Gossip is told "by whoever comes". What if that's the patron the gossip is about? | Patrons never pass on gossip about their own outcome; it waits for the next visitor. | open |
| Q12 | "A page whose tags meet goal.tags": does a page need any of the goal's tags, or all of them? | Any of them, at or above the goal's power. Every goal written so far has a single tag, so the two readings agree. | open |
| Q13 | What shape is `outcomes_generic.json`, and which of its outcomes gets used? | It's flat, keyed like visit outcomes: `good`, `unhelpful`, `harmMisuse`, `harmAccident`, `mixedMisuse`, `mixedAccident`, each a list. One entry is picked by a seeded draw and recorded in the event (`generic:harmMisuse:1`). | open |
| Q14 | When exactly does a `return` outcome become a letter? | If the patron has no slot on or after the surface day, it's redirected straight away to a letter on the morning after the surface day. If their slot comes and they don't, it becomes a letter the next morning. If the week ends first, its text appears in the week summary under "late word". | open |
| Q15 | The notebook header's "number of visits": does that include visits before the game? | It counts in-game visits plus one per pre-game loan on the card. Ada Marsh starts at 4. | open |
| Q16 | What does the week summary's "never heard" count? | Outcomes of loans that never surfaced. Declines and walk-outs are silent by default and aren't counted. | open |
| Q17 | Two lines the content had no place for: what the curator says when the requested book is out, and what Read Thoughts shows when nothing surfaces (a warded mind). | Added `bookOut` and `noThoughts` to `questions.json` → `genericLines`. Validation warns if they're missing. | open |
| Q18 | Identify needs the book "not locked", but no content marks a book as locked. | Not implemented. The locked shelf is scenery in the prototype (GDD §9). | settled |
| Q19 | A good outcome that surfaces in the morning (newspaper or letter): does it add to that same morning's mana? §5.2 says "good outcomes surfaced since the previous grant"; §5.11 says "for tomorrow's mana". | It counts for that morning, following §5.2's formula: morning news arrives before the grant (§5.1, steps 3 and 5). Outcomes heard during the day count for the next morning under both readings. | open |
| Q20 | If a question pushes a patron past their patience, which trust level answers it? | The lowered one: the cost comes first, then the answer. An annoyed patron says less. | open |
| Q21 | How far does "forced" go (A9)? | A forced visit can't be declined, can't be offered an alternative, and never ends in a walk-out: at zero trust the patron simply waits. Closing the library is also refused while a forced visit is still due that day. | open |
| Q22 | §5.11 lists `bookReturns` and `returnInDays` under surfacing, but they change the loan. | They apply at lending, when the due date is set (§5.10). | settled |
| Q23 | Two readings in the careful bot (§8.4): what is "casts Read Thoughts when mana allows", and what is "the best same-topic book with no danger seen"? | Read Thoughts only once enough mana is kept back for two Identifies in every visit still to come today. Without that, the third patron of the day always got a blind lend. The "best" book is the one known safest (most pages read with no danger), then the cheapest, since "the dangerous grimoire is often the lucrative one" (A13). The bot identifies only the requested book, as specified. | open |
| Q24 | Some desk text has no content home yet: the Identify-failed line, the "won't come into focus" note, and the gentle lines for refused commands. | M4 adds them to a content file rather than writing them into Godot code. | open |
